# SCOUT — candidate discovery skill 01

Find the material that can change the research decision, with enough recall to avoid a misleading first hit. Discovery has no claim-admission authority.

## Search by the information gap

- State the missing information and the decision it could change. Identify required source roles, date/version, population, method or scope; distinguish required coverage from optional inspiration.
- Inspect accessible canonical holdings and previously verified source identities first when applicable. Reuse them within their verified scope. If an original is already held, read the relevant original rather than downloading duplicates or relying on a summary.
- Search exact names, DOI or other stable identities, aliases, competing terminology and adverse results. Use parallel independent queries where available and useful. For difficult questions vary source families or research traditions; for a structurally stuck problem consider distant-domain method donors, then test the translation rather than treating analogy as proof.
- Resolve the original and its exact edition. Distinguish a search hit, metadata, abstract, accessible full text, actually inspected passages, and raw-data verification. Search rank, number of mirrors and retrieval success are not evidence quality. Deduplicate exact verified identities, retain different editions and unresolved possible duplicates, and preserve distinct original source roots. Do not infer independent witnesses from different URLs.
- Read the relevant methods, assumptions, results and limitations; record what each selected source supports and does not support. For tool discovery, distinguish registered, exposed, authorized, invoked and verified capabilities. Check real available access before promising execution.
- Stop when the necessary roles and uncertainty are adequately addressed or the budget is exhausted. Record unfilled roles, searched terms/surfaces and access blockers; bounded search absence is not global absence. Do not let search volume replace the research question.

## Result

Return a concise decision-linked source set with identity/version, provenance, access/read status, applicable scope, limitations and unresolved debt. Respect source rights, canonical custody and user-specific retrieval rules. No unsupported citations, invented direct-download links, or authority upgrades. If a required original is unavailable, state HOLD for that evidence role; optional exploration may proceed with explicit limits. Do not silently substitute another version or dataset.
