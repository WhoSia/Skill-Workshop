# COURT — candidate claim adjudication skill 01

Determine exactly what the available evidence warrants. Do not use adjudication as a substitute for producing the research result, or require final-publication rigor for ordinary exploratory learning.

## Adjudicate the claim, not the activity

- Identify the exact claim, scope, declared criterion, evidence requirements and authority ceiling. For prospective tests check that criteria were fixed before the relevant outcomes; retrospective analyses may be useful but must not be presented as prospective success.
- Resolve the source roots, revisions, actual observations, independence limitations and material changes since any inherited verdict. Check the evidence against the actual claim, not against a weaker surrogate. Separate permission, tool execution, artifact integrity, implementation correctness, formal validity, empirical utility, generalization and novelty.
- Inspect decisive counterexamples and the strongest relevant baseline. If a witness falsifies the exact claim, reject it at that scope even when other components succeeded. If required evidence or scope is unresolved, hold the affected claim. Do not erase a valid negative result by renaming a task or revising criteria after outcomes.
- PASS only the supported claim at its verified ceiling. HOLD means the stated evidence requirement is unresolved, not that the claim is false. REJECT means the declared claim or criterion failed under the stated evidence; preserve successful narrower claims independently. Do not infer a broader verdict from the number of passing tests.
- For consequential reuse, check the actual evidence read-set and active justification. If a trusted local certificate is stale or missing, revalidate the affected claim rather than redoing every unrelated experiment. A structural certificate is not a verdict and does not validate caller-supplied admission or the underlying science.
- Return the decisive reason, known failure boundary and smallest next action that can resolve the remaining uncertainty. Do not perform a ceremonial court when no authority decision is needed.

## Result

Give a claim-scoped verdict, criterion, evidence, scope, unsupported extensions and any unresolved obligations. If using 0–1 evidence, support, generalization or priority estimates, define them separately and explain the strongest limiting fact. Scores cannot override required evidence or a counterexample.
