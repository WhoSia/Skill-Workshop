# Continuity — recover, reuse and resume

**Find prior work (ATLAS).** Recover the smallest relevant canonical history: current question, assumptions, results, failed attempts, decisions and open obligations. Follow source identity and successor relationships, not recency alone. Preserve distinct branches and don't count copies as independent witnesses. Update authorized canonical objects rather than creating competing sources.

**Reuse (CACHE).** Match the actual contract, scope, version and complete material dependency set. Revalidate changed or uncertain roots before consequential reuse. A projection cannot certify itself. Check conflicting current declarations rather than selecting the newest summary. Preserve unaffected work and rebuild only affected parts. A stale or inaccessible result is not a fulfilled prerequisite.

**Preserve origins (NAPKIN capability).** Existing accessible chronological records can preserve original wording and distinctions. Bind to the actual pre-formalization source when it is needed; if the original is unavailable, do not retrofit a reconstruction as an original. Keep new interpretations separate. Decompose large work into bounded slices without changing the goal. No standalone note is needed when the existing record suffices.

**Trace (TRACE).** Keep a compact factual receipt linking input identity, action, observed outcome, output locator, integrity checks and unresolved failures. Distinguish execution, evidence, handoff and delivery. Compress only if the recipient can recover the authoritative record. Never fabricate a receipt, or store credentials or private reasoning.

**Resume.** Checkpoint meaningful durable progress before a costly or consequential blocking operation. Preserve the goal, completed outputs, open blockers, exact head and next action. If interface, environment or measurement conditions changed, use [Transition](transition.md) to decide the inheritance boundary. An unverified change does not justify same-run continuation. A checkpoint is not a scientific verdict. Do not silently alter existing Research OS contracts.
