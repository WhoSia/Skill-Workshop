# Discovery — retrieve useful evidence

**Discovery (SCOUT).** Search current canonical holdings first when applicable. Seek original sources and relevant competing work; expand by aliases or missing evidence, not hit count. Distinguish retrieved metadata, accessible original, admitted evidence and unresolved access. Report what a source supports and does not support.

**Method transfer.** When the existing approach stalls, search by the mathematical or engineering structure of the bottleneck, not only its domain vocabulary. Look for an existing theorem, algorithm, library or workflow with the same relevant structure. Map its inputs, assumptions and guarantee to the target; isolate mismatches and compare the smallest executable adaptation against the current approach. If the mapping fails, keep the mismatch as a clue rather than importing the donor's conclusion. Prefer a reusable working method over a bibliography; stop discovery once an adequate next action is available.

Return the useful result or obstruction, scope, assumptions, and next decisive observation or computation. Do not invoke every method or add a new claim of novelty.
