# Authoring — evidence-bound communication

Use when the deliverable is a manuscript, report, explanation, argument or substantial revision. Ordinary writing needs no separate planning or evaluation.

**Plan (RHETOR).** Start with audience, question, author intent and required claims. Arrange claims by what the reader needs to know and which claim supports the next; put definitions before dependencies and distinguish evidence, inference, examples and objections. Preserve required evidence pointers and author anchors. A missing premise is a research gap, not an invitation to invent support. Return a plan only if the task requests one; otherwise write the deliverable.

**Edit (HUMANIZER).** Preserve meaning, qualifiers, evidence, citations, exact quotations, protected literals, autobiographical facts and terminology. Match the requested language, genre and register. Prefer natural target-language syntax and explicit logical relations; merge redundant sentences and remove empty transitions. Keep author choices rather than importing style from unrelated contexts. If fluency conflicts with a protected distinction, preserve the distinction and repair the sentence. Do not invent experiences or infer authorship from style. No detector-evasion or authorship guarantees.

**Compare (KWRITE).** When actual candidate comparison is requested, bind candidates to the same source and protected claims; assess factual and semantic preservation before style. Reject material preservation failures regardless of fluency. For consequential preference evaluation, blind identities, balance order (AB/BA for paired candidates), keep private corpus material within its authorized scope, and record actual evaluator outputs and revision burden. A prepared packet is not an evaluation result. Do not fabricate preferences; mark absent results unresolved. KWRITE evaluates rather than generates prose.

Compose planning, editing and comparison only as needed. A routine rewrite may use direct editing alone. Return the requested text first, with material unresolved factual or preservation issues when relevant. No rhetorical improvement increases evidential authority.
